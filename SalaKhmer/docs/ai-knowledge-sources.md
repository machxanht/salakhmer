# SalaKhmer AI knowledge-source policy

The assistant uses short, reviewed and paraphrased knowledge cards. It does not
receive raw textbooks, scraped social-media posts, or an unrestricted web feed.

## Approved for curriculum guidance

- Owner-provided reference PDFs: used only to create paraphrased teaching facts;
  no book page, dialogue, image, or long passage is shipped to the Worker.
- **Basic Khmer**, Vathanak Sok, Michigan State University (2022), CC BY 4.0:
  its beginner topic framework is used with attribution. Source:
  <https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks/textbooks/basic-khmer>.

## Not imported into the production knowledge base

- Scribd and Facebook links without an exact item and an explicit reuse licence.
- **Intermediate Khmer** because it is CC BY-NC 4.0, incompatible with a future
  commercial/ad-supported SalaKhmer app without separate permission.
- khPOS because its published licence is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
- Kaikki/Wiktionary raw dumps. They are useful for research, but not a reviewed
  Khmer teaching corpus; their provenance/licensing and noisy senses must be
  resolved before any use.
- Materials attributed to Franklin E. Huffman until an exact edition and public
  domain/permission status has been verified.

## Intake rule

Every future card needs: exact source URL or owner file, licence/permission,
author or source name, a short paraphrase, its module/level, and Khmer-owner
review before deployment.
