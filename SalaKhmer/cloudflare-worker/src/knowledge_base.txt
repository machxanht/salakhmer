Global rules
- Khmer has no category called “independent consonants”.
- The SalaKhmer Reading Bridge is a beginner reading shortcut; it is not an exact Khmer spelling system.
- Do not invent a character, pronunciation, series, or category that is absent from this file.

LEVEL 3 — SUB-CONSONANTS (COENG)
Sub-consonants are smaller forms used below a main consonant in Khmer word building. They begin with the coeng sign ្.
Approved forms: ្ក ្ខ ្គ ្ឃ ្ង ្ច ្ឆ ្ជ ្ឈ ្ញ ្ដ ្ឋ ្ឌ ្ឍ ្ណ ្ត ្ថ ្ទ ្ធ ្ន ្ប ្ផ ្ព ្ភ ្ម ្យ ្រ ្ល ្វ ្ស ្ហ ្អ.

LEVEL 5 — INDEPENDENT VOWELS (15)
ឥ: I / Ih — like “i” in “it”.
ឦ: Ee — like “ee” in “see”.
ឧ: U — like “u” in “put”.
ឨ: Uu — like “oo” in “boot”.
ឩ: Oo — like “oo” in “cool”.
ឪ: Ov / Au — like “oh” blending into “v”, or “ow”.
ឫ: Rue — like “rue” with a rolled R.
ឬ: Ruee — long “rue”, heavily rolled R.
ឭ: Lue — like “loo”.
ឮ: Luee — long “loo”.
ឯ: Eh / Ae — like “ai” in “air”.
ឰ: Ai — like “i” in “hi”.
ឱ: O — like “o” in “so”.
ឲ: Aoy / Ao — like “oy” in “boy”.
ឳ: Au — like “ow” in “cow”.
